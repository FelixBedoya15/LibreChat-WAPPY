# WAPPY Blog Publishing & Content Repurposing Skill

This skill guides the end-to-end creation of articles for the WAPPY blog, optimized for search engines (SEO), AI Answer Engines (AEO - Perplexity, ChatGPT Search, Gemini), and social distribution.

---

## 1. Article Architecture

Every WAPPY blog post must contain:
1. **Compelling Title:** Clear value proposition including key phrase (e.g., *"Guía Práctica: Cómo Auditar la Matriz IPVER con Inteligencia Artificial"*).
2. **Direct Answer Block (AEO Hook):** A concise 2-3 sentence paragraph at the top directly answering the primary question, ideal for AI citations.
3. **Structured Body Sections (H2 & H3):**
   - Practical steps with bullet points.
   - Comparison tables or code/regulatory snippets.
   - Callout tips and important notes.
4. **Visual Diagram / Infographic Plan:** Mermaid diagram or prompt for cover image creation.
5. **Call to Action (CTA):** Encouraging the reader to try WAPPY agents or explore the LMS.

---

## 2. Multi-Channel Repurposing Checklist

Whenever an article is drafted:
- [ ] **LinkedIn Post / Carousel:** 5-7 slide summary focusing on problem-solution-action.
- [ ] **WhatsApp Broadcast Message:** Short, friendly 3-bullet takeaway with a link.
- [ ] **Image Prompt:** Prompt designed for Imagen / DALL-E in 16:9 aspect ratio representing the topic with WAPPY modern tech branding.
